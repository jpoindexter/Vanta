# STRATEGY.md — Vanta

> Current governing strategy. Anchored in the 2026-06-04 full-capability curb-cut decision and clarified by the append-only 2026-07-30 decision. `MANIFESTO.md` remains the human-authored north star.

## Product

**Vanta is a full-capability, life-integrated, progressively autonomous personal AI operator for the general human experience. It can do the broad work expected of a Hermes/OpenClaw-class agent, while specializing in trusted continuity and responsibility transfer when human attention, memory, time, and executive function are finite. Neurodivergent and disability experience supplies curb-cut universal-design requirements without limiting the audience.**

Broad research, browser, files, documents, coding, communication, scheduling, life administration, employment, business, relationships, distribution, media, devices, extensibility, and automation capability is the floor. No enumerated workflow limits the product.

The wedge is not feature count or a narrow vertical. Vanta owns the thread: understand the outcome, preserve state, choose or prepare the next safe step, act within authority, wait, follow up, recover, and truthfully close with evidence.

## Universal-design operating model

Executive capacity is finite and variable for everyone. Stress, sleep, illness, pain, aging, caregiving, parenting, grief, disability, workload, unfamiliar situations, and interruption all change what a person can carry.

Use neurodivergent and disabled experience as paid co-design input and non-negotiable source requirements. Make the resulting curb cuts—capture without taxonomy, concrete representation, one recommendation, time and transition support, low-friction initiation, re-entry, quiet proactivity, safe delegation, and shame-free recovery—available to everyone by default.

Do not infer diagnosis, require disclosure, create a paternalistic “ND mode,” or treat one support pattern as universal. Preferences and situational supports are negotiated, visible, scoped, correctable, expiring where appropriate, and immediately dismissible.

### Complete accessibility and support contract

- Never depend on “visualize the outcome.” Use concrete previews, examples,
  frames, timelines, before/after states, ordered steps, observable attributes,
  literal spatial descriptions, and durable references.
- Never encode meaning by color alone. Pair color with text, icons, shapes,
  position, and programmatic labels; support high contrast, common color-vision
  deficiencies, and reduced motion.
- Avoid forced streaming and auto-scroll; preserve reading position and stable
  layout; put summaries before detail.
- Capacity is multidimensional: cognitive, attentional, sensory, social,
  emotional, physical, and time-related. It may be `unknown`.
- Transient observations expire or require review. Preferences and capacity do
  not silently become permanent identity.
- Literal controls include `do it`, `show me`, `snooze`, `skip`, and `off`.
  Refusal works for the current suggestion/session, the repeated
  pattern/workflow, and global support.
- Quiet hours and interruption budgets are first-class. Honor refusal without
  bargaining, escalation, shame, nagging, or resurfacing the same suggestion
  through another subsystem.
- Use non-coercive language. Preserve safe, neutral accessibility when optional
  support is off. Never impose streak, backlog, guilt, or catch-up punishment.
- Preserve exact resume context through interruption, shutdown, failure,
  waiting, and variable capacity. Support initiation, transitions, stopping,
  recovery, and return without transcript reconstruction.

### Human relationship and restored-authority boundaries

Vanta complements people; it does not replace, monopolize, isolate, or
impersonate them. Trusted-person collaboration remains horizon-only until it has
granular bilateral consent; private/shared boundaries; provenance and
authorship; burden and notification controls; exact role and authority scope;
review, revocation, and expiry; visibility into what was shared and why; and
safeguards against coercion, surveillance, triangulation, and one-sided
authority.

Portable personal-data export may carry user-owned records and artifacts. It
must not silently restore or transfer credentials, OAuth refresh tokens,
approval authority, standing grants or capabilities, signing material,
protected audit/control state, trusted-device status, or safety-root/policy
authority. A destination requires fresh account binding, review, and explicit
reauthorization.

## Autonomy

`R0`–`R5` are reserved exclusively for autonomy. They are not risk, severity,
impact, receipt, or consequence labels.

1. **R0 — Observe:** read, classify, and report; no mutation.
2. **R1 — Recommend:** identify the outcome and propose one next action; no mutation.
3. **R2 — Prepare:** create private, reversible drafts, tasks, notes, reminders, or isolated artifacts.
4. **R3 — Confirm:** show the exact action preview and require fresh one-use authority.
5. **R4 — Delegate:** run an allowlisted recurring workflow within explicit target, account, recipient, quota, budget, expiry, exclusions, cancellation, and review bounds.
6. **R5 — Autonomous delegate:** in a proven bounded domain, initiate, chain, coordinate, communicate with permitted parties, monitor, reconcile, follow up, and recover without per-step approval.

Safety enables this autonomy. Authority remains scoped, visible, revocable, budgeted, interruptible, evidence-backed, and automatically demoted after failure or drift. Vanta cannot expand its own authority or rewrite policy, credentials, evaluators, audit state, or the root of trust.

`E0`–`E5` is reserved for a future consequence classifier but is not yet an
implementation-usable runtime scale. Consequence never grants autonomy and no E
label substitutes for the kernel's independent `Allow | Ask | Block` decision.

## One product, three internal boundaries

- **Vanta** is the one customer-facing operator across supported Desktop, TUI/CLI, messaging, background, and future hosts. Those hosts share one identity, work state, memory contract, authority model, and receipts.
- **Vanta Engine** is the execution and extensibility substrate: policy, effects, models, tools, skills, plugins, MCP, jobs, triggers, workers, memory, and recovery. Its machinery remains safely available but stays out of low-burden default navigation.
- **Vanta Lab** contains factory, auto-research, tuning, swarms/organizations, self-modification, and speculative autonomy experiments. It is absent from production defaults, proposal-only where it can affect Vanta, and unable to change the trust boundary.

These are exposure and trust boundaries, not three products, brands, repositories, or roadmaps. Multi-agent fan-out serves one owner internally; it is not the public identity and does not imply multi-tenancy.

## Product exposure

The default shell is one conversation with three lightweight concepts: **Today, Inbox, Projects**. A contextual Review surface contains only Activity, Changes, Outputs, and Evidence/Approvals when attention is genuinely required.

Models, providers, tools, workers, schedules, profiles, modes, hosts, worktrees, runs, and pack machinery are selected by intent or revealed progressively. Simplifying exposure must not delete the representative generalist capability floor.

Capability packs begin as dormant manifests/projections over existing registries. Inactive packs contribute zero prompt fragments, tool schemas, workers, requested credentials, or persistent navigation. Physical extraction needs measured coupling or release-support evidence.

## Trust contract

The intended boundary remains:

- goal before tool;
- external content is data, never authority;
- consequential effects cross one trusted action gateway;
- credentials are least-privilege and brokered;
- approvals bind the exact actor, account, operation, normalized target and arguments, content, quota, expiry, nonce, state precondition, and compensation;
- WorkItem lifecycle is exactly `draft`, `queued`, `running`, `waiting`,
  `needs human`, `stopped`, `failed`, `unverified`, or `verified`;
- receipt/action dispositions such as `denied`, `expired`, `unknown`, and
  `compensated` never become WorkItem states;
- model prose cannot create a verified accomplishment;
- immediate stop, revocation, recovery, and provenance are non-negotiable.

## Build-order rule

Build the smallest dependency-ready slices. Start with:

1. the smallest urgent hook, environment, authentication, and control-plane trust closure;
2. a read-only/local messy input → Today/prepared action → durable waiting/resume → restart/re-entry slice with no consequential external effect.

Next comes exact action/receipt binding, one canonical work facade, trustworthy Needs You, quarantined read-only morning orientation, and first-run/accessibility proof. Consequential email, calendar, messaging, publication, financial, and factory effects wait for their specific trust dependencies.

Every new item must name the user burden reduced, closed journey advanced, existing surface hidden or replaced, and authoritative evidence required. If it adds a product noun without retiring exposure, it stays captured rather than entering build order.

## Product proof

Release and product claims require executed evidence at the level claimed:

- representative generalist tasks across research/browser, files/documents, coding, planning, communication/scheduling, business, and media/creative work;
- multi-step continuity comparisons for reminders, repeated context, classification and permission burden, restart/re-entry, durable follow-up, and verified closure;
- safety and accessibility proof on the actual supported path;
- external provider readback for external effects;
- market evidence for demand and pricing.

Documentation, a JSON parse, a green unit test, roadmap volume, downloads, stars, or model narration cannot prove usefulness, safety, autonomy, or market demand.
