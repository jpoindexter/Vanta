## Canonical statement

> **Vanta is a full-capability, life-integrated, progressively autonomous personal
> AI operator for the general human experience. It can do the broad work expected
> of a Hermes/OpenClaw-class agent, while specializing in trusted continuity and
> responsibility transfer when human attention, memory, time, and executive
> function are finite. Neurodivergent and disability experience supplies curb-cut
> universal-design requirements without limiting the audience.**

## Initial build-order contract

### Building lane 1 — TRUST-02

Close the smallest confirmed critical slice:

- protect hook and control-plane state;
- prevent same-run hook activation;
- sanitize shell/hook/code environments;
- deny ordinary access to credentials and audit keys;
- authenticate local Desktop/API instances;
- prove adversarial attempts produce no unmediated effect or secret exposure.

This does not claim the entire action gateway is complete.

### Building lane 2 — UX-03

Run one safe, local/read-only journey:

```text
messy real input from life or work
→ desired outcome and provenance
→ one Today recommendation
→ relevant generalist capability prepares an action
→ durable waiting/resume state
→ real restart
→ last verified state and one re-entry action
```

No consequential external effect is required or authorized. This is the first
value slice, not the autonomy ceiling.

### Next

1. TRUST-01 — inventory and mediate all effect paths.
2. TRUST-04 — one typed outcome/receipt contract across hosts and memory.
3. OP-01 — minimal canonical Outcome/WorkItem/Job/Trigger/Action/Receipt facade.
4. GROW-01 — manual functional-problem interviews, continuity-loop pilots, and
   price/support evidence.

## Phase-0 evidence lane

Begin without waiting for source implementation.

### Cohort

Recruit on observable executive burden, not diagnosis. Intentionally include
neurodivergent and disabled lived experience as paid co-design, plus situational
burden such as caregiving, aging, parenting, overload, study, illness, grief,
creative work, and demanding transitions.

### Evidence

Record:

- ten functional-problem interviews;
- five bounded continuity-loop pilots across real life and work;
- task domains and representative generalist capabilities used;
- assisted and unassisted runs separately;
- two-loop use in weeks three and four;
- burden before/after;
- prevented misses, restored work, and verified outcomes;
- payment or explicit price/value rejection;
- acquisition source;
- founder support minutes;
- safety incidents;
- reasons for non-return;
- a precommitted continue/change/park date and time/expense ceiling.

Unknown values remain `unproven`. Fixtures, roadmap cards, downloads, stars,
content impressions, and heuristic opportunity scores cannot close GROW-01.

## ND/EF, skills, PM behavior, and profile migration

- Universal support moves from turns/tools/Git proxies to desired outcome, owner,
  next action, blocker/waiting condition, time/capacity fit, drift, resume
  context, and truthful closure.
- Support must be available before action and through Desktop, TUI, messaging,
  background, restart, dismissal, and expiry.
- `do`, `show`, `not now`, `snooze`, `never for this pattern`, `skip`, `off`,
  `why`, and `unknown` are first-class.
- Aphantasia and color-vision requirements apply to generated artifacts as well
  as app chrome.
- Existing solutioning/research-to-offer/weekly-review/revenue/inspection
  behavior becomes Business/Growth recipes. No new PM persona, mode, or store.
- Skills are recipes, not runtime proof. New skills require deduplication,
  recurrence, trigger, owner, source/version, risk, host coverage, evaluation,
  usage evidence, and archive path.
- Dormant skills leave the default index while remaining searchable; measure
  index tokens, duplicate selection, and task success.
- Stale identity, profiles, skills, preferences, memories, and standing loops
  require a product workflow with backup, diff, explicit opt-in, provenance,
  rollback, and review.
- This realignment does not modify `~/.vanta` or any user runtime state.
