Canonical vocabulary

One autonomy ladder. One lifecycle.

R0–R5: autonomy only

  1. R0 — Observe: read, classify, report; no mutation.
  2. R1 — Recommend: outcome plus one next action; no mutation.
  3. R2 — Prepare: private reversible drafts or isolated artifacts.
  4. R3 — Confirm: exact preview plus fresh one-use authority.
  5. R4 — Delegate: allowlisted recurring workflow inside exact bounds.
  6. R5 — Autonomous delegate: proven bounded domain may chain, coordinate, communicate, monitor, reconcile, follow up, and recover.
